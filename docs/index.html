Kitab

A free, fast book reader for Android. Read EPUB, PDF and TXT, find free classics, and turn scanned old books into text you can read comfortably.

Free · no ads · no account · Android 5.0 or newer

How to use Kitab

1 · Find a bookTap Browse, search anything, tap a cover, tap Download. Free.
2 · Or bring your ownTap + Add and pick any EPUB, PDF or TXT from your phone.
3 · Just readTap the edges to turn pages. Tap the middle for the menu. That's all.
4 · Make it feel rightTap Aa. Pick a theme, a font, a size. Night mode is true black.
5 · Old scanned book?Tap Aa, then Scan text. Page pictures become real text you can resize.
6 · Eyes tired?Tap Aa, then Listen. Kitab reads to you and turns the pages.
7 · Come back any timeKitab remembers where you stopped, in every book.
No ads. No account.Your books and progress stay on your phone.

Install in 3 taps

  1. Tap Download Kitab on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file Kitab.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.
On a computer? Scan with your phone.
