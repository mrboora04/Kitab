Free book websites

Places that share books you can read for free

Every site listed offers books that are free and legal to read: public domain, open access, or openly licensed. Tapping a site opens it in your browser. Kitab is not connected to these sites and hosts no books. Sites with a download arrow have a source you can install, so their books appear inside Kitab.

Extensions

Add sources of free, public books

Repositories

A repository is one link to a list of sources. Sources are small recipes, not apps: they cannot run code, and each one can only contact the websites it lists. Kitab checks every source against the list before installing it. Sources offer free, public or open-access books only.

Eye care

Kitab counts only the time you actually read. Every reminder suggests the 20-20-20 habit: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Privacy

No ads, no account, no tracking. Kitab only uses the internet when you browse catalogs, and your books and notes never leave your phone.
